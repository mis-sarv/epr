Approval Rules

A Dispatch Packing Slip needs approval by its weight difference (Kg) = Net Wt (Gross − Tare) minus the Grand Total of the ERP packing slips loaded on the tempo (either way); with no packing slip data the Net Wt itself is used. Each band runs from its From up to (not including) its To; the last band has no To (= and above). Every person of the band's group gets the request on the launcher; the first to approve / reject with their PIN decides. The same rules work for both gates.

Gross Wt auto-lock timer

Once the guard types the Gross Wt on the Dispatch Packing Slip, the box locks by itself after these seconds (0 = never). Choosing the Dharam Kanta Slip again opens it.
