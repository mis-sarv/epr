ⓘ How to read this report
Numbers are shown in short form (PR/26-27/608 → PR608, PO/U-2/793 → PO793, SIHF-II/2026-27/1913 → 1913, NB/2/2026-27/1250 → NB1250) — the full number shows on hover, and clicking opens that document's PDF.
When a PR has several POs, or a PO has several Inwards, each PO / Inward gets its own row; the PR and PO columns stay merged across those rows.
Only Inform to Requester is ticked by hand. It needs your role's PIN, and once ticked it is locked; removing it needs an Admin login + Admin PIN. MD Approved, PO Sent to Vendor and Bill Checked by GM / MD update automatically from the mobile app's PO Sender and Store Check.
Delivery Revise & Remarks : when the PO Sender, in the mobile app's PO Follow-up tab, records the vendor's new delivery date, it shows here (latest first). Click History in the cell to see every follow-up entry: date, remark, who made it and when. The PO's original Delivery Date of PO does not change; it stays what was sent to the vendor.
Click a PO Status badge (or PR details where there is no PO) for the full PR → PO → Inward view: approval journey, item-wise ordered / received / pending, every receipt with its sign-offs, and the vendor follow-up history. While typing in Search, matching values from every column are suggested below the box.
